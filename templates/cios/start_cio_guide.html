How to Start a CIO at UVA

A step-by-step guide to creating a Contracted Independent Organization at the University of Virginia.

Before you start: Check the Gauge Interest page to see if others share your idea, or propose your own to find potential co-founders.

Start by clearly defining the mission and purpose of your organization. Consider what gap your CIO will fill in the UVA community.

  • Identify your organization's mission, goals, and target audience
  • Research existing CIOs to make sure your idea is not duplicated
  • Find at least 2-3 committed co-founders to help get things started
  • Use the Gauge Interest feature to see if there is demand for your idea

UVA requires all CIOs to have a minimum of 10 currently enrolled student members to be recognized.

  • Spread the word through friends, classmates, and social media
  • Host an informal interest meeting to gauge support
  • Collect names and email addresses of interested students
  • All members must be currently enrolled UVA students

Every CIO must have a written constitution that outlines how the organization will operate. UVA Student Activities provides templates to help.

  • Include the organization name, purpose, and mission statement
  • Define membership eligibility and requirements
  • Outline officer positions, election procedures, and terms
  • Describe meeting frequency and decision-making processes
  • Include an anti-discrimination and anti-hazing statement
  • Specify amendment procedures for the constitution
  • All CIOs must comply with UVA's non-discrimination policies

UVA requires each CIO to have at least one faculty or staff advisor who is a current University employee.

  • Reach out to professors or staff members who align with your mission
  • The advisor must be a current, full-time UVA faculty or staff member
  • The advisor serves as a mentor and liaison with the University
  • Advisors do not need to attend every meeting but should be available for guidance

Submit your registration through UVA's Office of Student Activities and Involvement using the Contracted Independent Organization Agreement.

  • Complete the CIO registration form on the Student Activities website
  • Upload your constitution
  • Provide the names of at least 10 members and your faculty advisor
  • Designate at least 2 officers (President and Treasurer at minimum)
  • All designated officers must complete required training modules
  • Accept the terms of the CIO Agreement with the University
Important: CIOs are independent entities, not official University of Virginia organizations. The CIO Agreement outlines the terms under which the University permits the use of its name, facilities, and resources.

Officers of newly registered CIOs must complete mandatory training before the organization becomes fully active.

  • Complete the online CIO Officer Orientation modules
  • Attend any required in-person sessions offered by Student Activities
  • Review policies on event planning, funding, and risk management
  • Understand UVA's policies on hazing prevention and alcohol use

Once approved, it is time to build your community and make an impact!

  • Create your CIO profile on this Clearinghouse to attract members
  • Attend the Student Activities Fair at the beginning of each semester
  • Host events, meetings, and outreach to grow your membership
  • Apply for funding through Student Council or your school's student government
  • Book rooms through 25Live for meetings and events
  • Remember to re-register your CIO each academic year to stay active
